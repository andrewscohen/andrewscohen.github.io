Your lobby is the front line of building safety. It is where residents, guests, deliveries, and service providers all intersect — and where unauthorized entry, loitering, package theft, and nuisance activity can quickly become daily problems. Fact is, most incidents start at the front door.

Video Doorman Safe Lobby helps multifamily properties take better control of that environment without the cost of expanding full-time on-site staffing. With 24/7 monitored coverage, live verified response, and smart detection, property teams gain better visibility into suspicious activity at the front entrance and surrounding common areas.

From the vestibule and intercom entry to package rooms and lobby traffic, the service is designed to help identify issues earlier, verify events in real time, and support faster action when safety concerns arise. The result is a more secure resident entry experience, better protection for deliveries and common spaces, and a more consistent way to manage front-door activity across the building.

Whether the challenge is repeated loitering, non-resident traffic, package-room misuse, or gaps in after-hours oversight, Video Doorman Safe Lobby provides a practical way to incorporate existing infrastructure, improve safety, and reduce risk while keeping operating costs predictable.
