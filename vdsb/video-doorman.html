In today’s rental market, tenants want more than just an apartment — they want convenience, control, and a building that feels easier to live in. Video Doorman helps non-doorman properties compete more effectively by delivering services that make everyday life smoother for residents while improving how the building operates.

With Video Doorman, buildings can accept packages from major carriers like UPS, FedEx, USPS, and Amazon, as well as deliveries from local businesses and service providers. Residents gain the convenience of a more professionally managed entry experience, while owners and managers gain a practical way to make the property more attractive without taking on the cost of full-time lobby staffing.

In addition to package handling, Video Doorman supports visitor management, monitored entry, and better oversight of lobby activity — helping create a building experience that feels more secure, more organized, and more desirable to current and prospective tenants.
