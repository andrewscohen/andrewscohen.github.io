INCLUDES SAFE LOBBY
Every Safe Building® package includes full Safe Lobby® coverage, plus stairwell and common-area monitoring layered on top.

Building safety does not begin and end at the lobby. Many of the biggest risks in multifamily properties happen across the rest of the building — at side entrances, garages, package rooms, shared hall access points, and other common areas that are difficult to staff and monitor consistently.

Video Doorman Safe Building® gives property owners and managers a broader security approach that helps protect residents, control unauthorized access, and improve visibility across the property. By combining 24/7 monitored coverage, live viewing and verified response, with smart AI detection, the service helps teams identify suspicious activity faster and respond more effectively to loitering, trespassing, after-hours access issues, and common-area safety concerns.

Instead of relying only on limited on-site coverage, properties gain a more consistent layer of monitored protection across high-risk areas. That means better oversight of who enters the building, stronger awareness of nuisance activity, improved incident documentation, and a more scalable way to support safety without dramatically increasing labor costs.

For multifamily operators, Video Doorman Safe Building® offers a practical way to strengthen building-wide security, improve resident confidence, and bring more control to the areas where incidents are most likely to happen.
