Crofton’s neighborhood table

Food, music, community

Fresh food for the way Crofton gathers.

Ashling Kitchen & Bar is our casual-but-elevated neighborhood restaurant and bar, created around delicious, fresh food and a guest experience that never takes shortcuts.

The menu is built to welcome different palates, pairing diverse and international flavors with accessible, familiar American cuisine. Whether the table is set for family, friends, or a night out, food is the foundation for the moment.

The restaurant is also our home base for the event and entertainment culture that shaped Ashling Hospitality Group. Here, dinner, drinks, music, and community share the same room.

Live at Ashling

Make dinner a night out.

Our in-house events programme brings menus, cocktails, and live entertainment together in the heart of Crofton.

The in-house programme

Crofton gets a stage of its own.

In 2022, Marketing Director Hope recognized an opportunity to bring unforgettable events to the restaurant. Live at Ashling grew from that idea.

Each event is considered as a complete night: a handcrafted menu, specialty cocktails, and an ambiance shaped to match the theme. The calendar makes room for spectacle, laughter, mystery, and a dinner worth lingering over.

The Star Room is our private event space, carrying the same restaurant hospitality and entertainment point of view into gatherings of your own.
