Hospitality, born backstage

Our beginning

This story began behind the curtain.

Owner Elise spent more than a decade working in the music industry, including six years as Mac Miller’s Tour Manager. Life on the road made one truth clear: a good meal, a stiff drink, and thoughtful care can create community wherever you are.

In 2016, a call from the production manager at the Lyric Theater in Baltimore became the beginning of Ashling Market and Catering. Elise and partner Saeed built the business around backstage hospitality, attention to detail, and food that could feel diverse and familiar, sophisticated and accessible.

That point of view now moves through Ashling Hospitality Group—from touring productions across the Baltimore–Washington metropolitan area to weddings, private events, and our Crofton restaurant.
