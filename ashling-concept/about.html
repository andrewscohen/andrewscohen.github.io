What holds us together

We’re not fancy. We’re family.

For us, hospitality is personal. It is the calm backstage, the celebration around a table, and the familiar welcome waiting in Crofton. Every part of the work begins with making people feel cared for.

Hospitality, born backstage

Our beginning

The story began behind the curtain.

Owner Elise spent more than a decade in the music industry, including six years as Mac Miller’s Tour Manager. Traveling the world made the value of community clear: a good meal, a stiff drink, and thoughtful care can create a sense of home wherever you are.

In 2016, the production manager at the Lyric Theater in Baltimore asked Elise to help elevate the backstage experience for artist clients. She and her partner Saeed launched Ashling Market and Catering around that opportunity and their shared vision.

They built a reputation for superior hospitality, close attention to detail, and food that could feel diverse and familiar, sophisticated and accessible. Today, that kitchen continues to serve entertainment artists at venues throughout the Baltimore–Washington metropolitan area.

The same instinct now runs through Ashling Hospitality Group: backstage production hospitality, weddings and events, and Ashling Kitchen & Bar in Crofton.

The Food

Life’s moments belong on the table.

Food gives us a sense of home and community. It belongs at the dinner table with family, beside friends enjoying a winning season, and at the center of a private celebration.

Our kitchen brings diverse and international flavors to accessible, familiar American cuisine. Whatever the room, we want the food to become a foundation for experiencing life together.

The Ashling Experience

The energy of a show, right here in Crofton.

In 2022, Marketing Director Hope began our in-house events program in Crofton. Hypnotist shows, comedy sets, burlesque, murder mystery dinners, wine dinners, and drag shows pair handcrafted menus and specialty cocktails with an atmosphere shaped for the night. The Star Room gives private events a place of their own.

The Team

Many backgrounds. One instinct for care.

Our backgrounds reach across the music industry, small business ownership, hospitality management, fine dining, and nonprofit culinary training programs. That range helps us meet each room with both skill and humanity.

Our wider team

Many backgrounds, one table.

Our collective experience includes hospitality management, fine dining, music, entrepreneurship, and nonprofit culinary training. Each perspective adds something to the welcome.

In our community

Care goes beyond the table.

Hospitality means looking after the community around us, too. Our team supports school supply drives and on-the-job training opportunities for men and women in addiction recovery.

The work is another expression of the same belief that started backstage: people do better when they have a place to feel supported, welcomed, and at home.
