Our beginning

The road taught us the work.

Owner Elise spent more than a decade in the music industry, including six years as Mac Miller’s Tour Manager. On tour, she learned how a good meal, a stiff drink, and thoughtful care can create community wherever the show lands.

In 2016, the production manager at the Lyric Theater in Baltimore asked Elise to help elevate the backstage experience for artist clients. She and partner Saeed launched Ashling Market and Catering around that call.

They earned a reputation for superior hospitality, thoughtful attention to detail, and food that felt diverse and familiar, sophisticated and accessible. Today, that backstage instinct still guides Ashling Hospitality Group across the Baltimore–Washington metropolitan area.
